## Course summaries

### Claude Cowork 101 (`CK6LYT2APkKg`), updated 2026-08-06
Company-wide onboarding for Claude Enterprise. "Superside has moved to Claude as our company-wide AI platform. Every Supersider now has access", with no seat limits.
- **Access & modes:** the Claude desktop app is the expected default. It has three modes: Chat for everyday tasks, **Cowork** for files, tools, scheduled tasks and Slack/Drive work, and Code for terminal/dev work. "All Supersiders are encouraged to use Cowork."
- **Building blocks:** Projects (persistent context, named by client or work type), Files/Project Knowledge, Memory (off by default; turn on in Settings > Capabilities), Skills, Connectors (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Notion, Slack, Claude in Chrome), and Models (default Sonnet, Opus for complex work, Haiku for quick answers).
- **Folder setup:**
  1. Create a `Claude Cowork/` folder with `ABOUT ME/`, `OUTPUTS/` and `TEMPLATES/`.
  2. Have Claude interview you to write `about-me.md` (under 300 lines), `my-company.md` and `avoid-ai-writing.md` (installed via the Cowork skill).
  3. Add global instructions in Settings > General > Instructions for Claude.
  4. Connect the **parent** folder in Cowork and star it.
- **Prompting & context:** Role, Context, Task, Format. The bigger wins come from having context in place (About Me files, Project files, connectors).
- **Skills:**
  - Turn work that went well into a Skill with `/skill-creator`, or create one in Settings > Customize > Create a new skill. Build one after a task has worked well 3 or more times.
  - Superside team Skills live in the Cowork marketplace, so check there first.
  - Inspect external Skills' `.md` files before installing. Watch for instructions to fetch URLs, exfiltrate data, ignore other instructions or claim authority.
  - Share good Skills in #-s-everyone-can-build.
- **Use cases by role:** AM, PM/CL, Creative, Finance, People, Ops, Sales, Marketing prompt examples.
- **Responsible use:** see the Policies section below.
- **Tokens:** default to Sonnet at low or medium effort. Keep About Me files at 1,500–2,000 words. Start a new chat from a handover note when a chat gets long. Convert docs and PDFs to `.md`.

### The Claude Plugin for PMs (`fJxAbSndvGpf`), updated 2026-09-14
The PM plugin connects Claude to live Superspace data (projects, briefs, quotes, accounts, client chat) within your existing permissions.
- **Setup:**
  1. Claude app > Customize > Connectors > install **Superside Internal MCP**.
  2. Customize > Plugins > Browse > **Your Org** tab > install the **PM plugin**.
  3. Also connect Asana, Slack and Notion.
- **Skills:**
  - `/morning-briefing`: on demand, or as a daily Slack DM set up with `/schedule` in Cowork. Covers deadline triage, 🔴🟡🟢 sentiment, unanswered mentions and staffing.
  - `/project-status`: pulls from Asana, the Slack channel and the last 24h of customer chat.
  - `/compose-chat-message`: drafts in your voice in a canvas and sends only after you confirm.
  - `/quoting`: builds a quote from the brief and the quoting catalog, with a visual artifact.
  - `/create-and-submit-project`
  - `/logging-management-hours`: categories are setup, review & QC, client & delivery, admin & internal.
  - `/new-project`: brief triage that produces a gaps card or hands off to plan publishing.
  - `/publish-project-plan`: checks the three pillars (brief, timeline, estimate) and CL/customer approval.
  - `/close-project`: ONGOING → CLOSED, only after you confirm.
  - Always-on `using-project-management`.
- About 80 underlying tools (Projects, Briefs, Assets, Quoting, Accounts, Memory, Staffing) answer plain-language questions. The full tool catalog is in Notion: "Claude Project Management plugin".
- **Recordings:** Session 1 Claude Basics, Session 2 Meet the Skills, Session 3 Full PM Lifecycle simulation.
- **Sandbox:** Superspace account "104597 - INTERNAL - 4.3 ANNUAL".
- The quick-reference card names skills slightly differently from the plugin itself (e.g. `/project-status` vs `get-project-status`, `/quoting` vs `quote-project`).

### Creative Superside Brand Models (`ewy1GcEdXsCy`) and Brand Models for AM/PMs (`JPanUigTssCh`), updated 2026-05-25
- **What a Brand Model is:** 5–50 images plus a description, with no training. The prompt is compared to the description and the most relevant dataset images are injected. Usable for styles, products, characters and combinations. Available through the Figma/FigJam plugin and Superspace.
- **Creatives course:**
  - Install the **internal** plugin version in Figma (Manage plugins). The other version is the customer plugin.
  - Create models under Models > New model.
  - Prompt with `@model`. Combine up to 3 models.
  - Advanced settings: context count, base model (Nano Banana Pro/2), 3K/4K output, up to 5 image inputs.
  - Models are available company-wide.
  - Publish a model by assigning it to an account, then toggling Published on that account's Brand Models tab in internal.superside.com.
  - Six use-case tutorials: setup, editing, product photography, style transformation, sketch-guided generation, vector generation.
- **AM/PM course:**
  - In Superspace, use the Omnibox > Context > "Use brand models" or "Refer past projects".
  - Positioning: Brand Models are "the image layer of the context intelligence" from client work, not a standalone magic product. Raw AI output may still need post-production.
  - Playground demo account via an access sheet.
  - Sales Pitch Guide, Brand Models and Brand Brain client decks, and a customer-facing video tutorial.
  - Support: #brand-models-support.

## Policies & approved tools

Consolidated from Claude Cowork 101 ("Responsible use", "Skills", "Tokens"), Creative tools at Superside ("Two kinds of tools", "Available on request", "Four things that carry real risk"), Upgrade Your Workday ("Most common AI concerns") and Creative AI PM ("AI FAQ").

**Policies to read**
- **Superside AI Acceptable Use Policy:** linked from Claude Cowork 101 > Responsible use, together with Anthropic's usage policy.
- **AI FAQ sheet** (including legal aspects of using AI in creative work): linked from Creative AI PM: Identifying Opportunities.
- **Responsible AI team's Safe AI Usage Guide:** referenced in Upgrade Your Workday With AI.
- **Live tool list:** Notion "Creative Tooling at Superside". Ask the Risk team about any tool you are unsure of.

**Claude / data handling**
- Superside uses Claude's **enterprise tier**. Conversation content is **not used to train models**, but **usage data may be visible to admins** for compliance monitoring.
- Cowork only reads the folders you explicitly select.
- Review every output before it reaches a client. Cross-check facts and figures. "You are responsible for what you send."
- Rule of thumb: if you wouldn't send it to a personal email, don't paste it into Claude.
- Only install Skills from trusted sources. Start with the Superside Cowork marketplace, and read external Skills' `.md` files for prompt-injection or exfiltration instructions.
- Don't buy personal AI subscriptions for work. Claude is assigned at onboarding, and personal accounts are "a duplicate cost and a compliance risk".

**Centrally managed vs individual tools**
- AI tools, Figma, Canva, Claude and stock are **centrally managed** by Creative Ops. The four reasons:
  - AI accounts are configured for customer and legal privacy and model-training requirements.
  - Shared delivery environments.
  - Commercial licensing.
  - Customer-mandated environments.
- Adobe CC and MS Office are individually bought.

**Not approved (Legal review)**
- **Kling, ByteDance tools and CapCut, used directly**, are not approved for Superside delivery.
- The same models *are* fine inside Superside-managed platforms such as Krea or Flora, because the licensing and data terms sit with the platform.
- The list changes as Legal reviews more tools.
- Don't onboard a new vendor mid-project for a centrally managed tool.

**Other guardrails seen**
- Upgrade Your Workday (generic template): keep sensitive customer and employee data in trusted systems, and report accidental exposure to the security team. It singles out Gemini as not built for secure use with customer data.
- QC: AI images must be checked for copyrighted content and trademarks, and for bias and stereotypes (AI QC checklist).
- Brand Models: raw AI images may need post-production, so don't over-promise them to clients as a standalone solution.
