# AI, Briefly (weekly meeting)

**Recordings: Sana 'AI, Briefly' collection.** Each session is a self-paced Sana course titled "<N> / AI, Briefly - <Topic>", with the recording, slides and sometimes extra files. Courses #3 to #67 were found (65 courses). There are no courses #1 or #2: the numbering starts at #3, which is the launch session on 2025-06-18. Sana numbers and titles are treated as the authority below. Sessions #3 to #15 have Sana transcripts. From #16 onwards most recordings are Drive embeds without transcripts, so those summaries come from the decks (read through Drive) plus earlier Slack recaps.

**What it is:** A weekly, optional 15-minute live session ("a weekly 15-minute hit of AI inspiration"; "where we trick you into learning stuff"). Superside people share what's new at Superside, what's happening in AI, and tools, tips, case studies and team wins. It started on 2025-06-18 and the first session drew ~150 people. #50 was a special 1-hour edition on 2026-07-01 with community awards and the "#50 AI, Briefly Challenge" ($300 in prizes, run in Figma). From #55, "The Skill Drop" became a monthly edition about the Creative Skill Marketplace.

### #60, 2026-09-09: Video Production (Juan Esteban Charry)
- **Three ways to drive a shot**: prompt only, first + last frame, or an image/video reference.
- **Pre-production**: ships as one package (sketch storyboard, character contact sheets, location plates, props, locked style). Lock the look first, and sketch the board on purpose.
- **Production**: make 1:1 base images, generate the hero ratio first, then expand to every other ratio and check each crop. Fix framing with Smart Objects, flips and crops rather than regenerating.
- **Post-production**: overshoot (5 s for a 3 s need) and trim. Use multiple references with one job each: strip the plate, feed Seedance 360-degree car turnaround videos, and name roles with @image#. Borrow camera moves from a reference clip; Runway video expand is the fallback.

Sign it off as a still, then let it move.
