# AI, Briefly (weekly meeting)

**What it is:** A weekly, optional 15-minute live session ("a weekly 15-minute hit of AI inspiration") where Superside people share what's new at Superside, what's happening in AI, and tools, tips and team wins. Started 2025-06-18 (first session drew ~150 people). Session #50 was a special 1-hour edition on 2026-07-01, with the "#50 AI, Briefly Challenge" ($300 in prizes, run in Figma).

## Session summaries (newest first)

None of the Gemini notes could be opened. The summaries below come from readable primary material (one deck and one Notion outline) and from the organizers' Slack recaps and announcements.

### 2026-11-11: Creative Superskills (cancelled)
Planned hard launch and live demo of the **Creative Superskills** Claude plugin from Creative Factories. It covers First Draft (brief to three on-brand Figma proposals), AdGen (finished static ads), Ad Resizer, Figma Transcreation, and deck builders for Figma Slides, Google Slides and PowerPoint. The session was cancelled the same day ("we'll resume next week"). Optional enablement sessions are on 18 Nov (EMEA) and 25 Nov (LATAM).

### 2026-11-04: AI-built visual system (Jon Saude)
How Jon built a "stunning and solid system" with AI across the whole process: image generation, video generation and vibe-coding.

### 2026-10-28: Motion DNA (Gian Orsi, Aaron Amortegui)
**Motion DNA** is an After Effects plugin connected to Claude through MCP, built with Opus 5.5. It offers granular control over animation behaviours and easing, motion libraries, and predefined behaviours. Testing happens in #motion_dna_mcp_testing.

### 2026-10-15 and 2026-10-07: Claude Code for Design (Tor Egil Fusdahl; Manuel Berbin)
In #64, Tor Egil presented Claude Code as a new, accessible way to produce creative work, with big efficiency gains for production work. In part 2, "Dr. Stragentlove", Manuel shared a backbone/cheatsheet for working with agent models.

### 2026-09-02: Daily Habit, /daily-planning (Careth Colmenarez, Matteo Rostagno)
Demo of the **/daily-planning** Claude skill, which pulls from Slack, Gmail, Asana, Superspace and Calendar. It shows top-5 priorities tagged Make / Unblock / Decide, missed threads, project rows, billable hours against target, the day's meetings and deadlines, and action buttons. It went live for everyone the next day in the Creative Starter Pack plugin.

### 2026-08-12: Keeping Color True (#56, Aaron Amortegui)
Fixing colour drift in batches of Nano Banana 2 images. Aaron showed the ComfyUI custom-node solution built for the Dick's e-commerce engine, plus a **colour-correction script** anyone can use. The script and the measured per-generation colour variations are in the Color_script Drive folder.

### 2026-07-08: Marqeta 3D asset library (Javier Velazquez, Sara Popov)
Source: the Notion presentation outline. The team **vibe-coded a Blender add-on** that turns SVG icons into controlled, repeatable, client-adjustable 3D renders. It covers extrusion, bevels, materials, lighting, camera and batch render. They built it with Claude/Cursor plus the **Blender MCP**. The thesis: use AI to automate the specialist tool that already gives control, rather than generating final images. The add-on runs locally with no per-asset token cost and is reusable by the client. The same pattern applies to Unity, TouchDesigner, Unreal, After Effects and C4D.

### 2026-07-01: #50 special edition (Kae Neskovic, Phillip Maggs)
One-hour celebration with the #50 Challenge winners. Phillip presented the **personal-voice-builder** skill, which learns your writing voice from your Slack messages and produces an installable skill plus an analysis report. Phillip had also planned a Superside MCP teaser.

### 2026-05-13: From Nodes to Image (Julia Calabrese)
Behind the scenes of a node-based image workflow in **Flora**. The Flora project was shared for reuse.

### 2026-04-29: The Playground Mindset (Flavio Treiger)
Seasonal AI image workflows for Dick's Sporting Goods' Walter Hagen golf apparel, part of the AI e-commerce engine that produced 929 retouched assets.

### 2026-03-25: Brand Brain and new Brand Models (Phillip Maggs, Manuel Berbin)
Live demo of Superspace 2.0 and the Superside Brand Brain, plus a walkthrough of the new Brand Model setup.
